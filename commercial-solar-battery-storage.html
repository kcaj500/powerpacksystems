Full System

Commercial solar & battery storage

Generate, store and use your own power.

Solar PV (Photovoltaic) and battery storage designed together around your business. Our Full System approach combines on-site generation with a purpose-built energy storage container, helping you use more of your solar and reduce the electricity you need to buy.

Discuss Your Project →

Solar and storage as one project

Starting from scratch gives us the opportunity to design solar generation, battery capacity and controls together. We assess when your business uses electricity and how much of the proposed solar generation it could use directly or store for later.

The aim is a system that suits your operating pattern. Solar output, available space, electricity demand and the grid connection all influence the proposed design.

Build the business case first

We review your consumption, tariff and planned changes alongside the proposed solar generation. For established sites, the previous 12 months of half-hourly usage is ideal. For new premises, projected demand and operating hours provide a starting point.

Your proposal sets out the equipment, project costs, projected savings and key assumptions. Savings depend on generation, the timing of demand, electricity prices and system losses. We explain those assumptions so you can assess the investment.

Designed for your site

We consider the proposed solar installation, site access, electrical integration and safety requirements during survey and design. The battery container is built and fitted out off site before installation, helping reduce work and disruption at your premises.

Where required, we coordinate the DNO (Distribution Network Operator) process and documentation. Any backup requirement is defined during design; solar and storage do not automatically provide uninterrupted power during an outage.

Common questions

How is Full System different from Fit in Line?

Full System combines a new solar installation and battery storage in one project. Fit in Line adds storage alongside an existing solar system. Explore storage for existing solar.

Will we still use electricity from the grid?

A grid-connected system can still import electricity when on-site generation and stored energy are insufficient. We assess how solar and storage can reduce that reliance around your business’s demand.

Can the system also provide backup?

Backup can be considered as part of the design. We need to establish the critical loads, required duration and changeover arrangements. Explore UPS and backup systems.

Do we need energy data before contacting you?

No. Start with your site, priorities and planned changes. We will explain what information is needed to assess the project.
