Off-grid & generator support

Off-grid solar & battery storage

Our off-grid solution brings together new or existing solar, battery storage and a suitable generator to power sites without a mains connection. We assess how your existing solar can work with the system, or design a new solar installation where needed, with the generator providing additional power and charging support.

The aim is to reduce unnecessary generator running and use stored energy when it makes sense for your site. Fuel savings and generator-off periods depend on your loads, equipment and operating strategy.

How solar, storage and a generator work together

Solar supplies daytime loads and charges the battery when generation exceeds demand. Stored energy can then supply agreed loads when solar output falls. The generator supports the system when solar and stored energy are insufficient, according to the agreed operating strategy.

We work with new or existing solar installations. We assess the existing equipment and generation, or the space available for new panels, alongside seasonal output, daily demand and essential loads. Battery storage and generator support are then designed around the site’s needs.

Less running time. More control over fuel costs.

For a site with varying demand, storage may reduce periods when a generator runs mainly to serve light loads. It can also create quieter periods while loads are supplied by the battery. Where fuel use falls, associated generator emissions can fall too.

We assess potential savings against your actual generator use and load profile, including charging losses and operating costs. A continuously high load may offer less opportunity to switch the generator off.

Start with the generator you already have

We review the existing generator’s rating, output, control capabilities and suitability for battery charging. Integration is assessed before equipment is specified. Automatic start and stop can be considered where the generator and controls support it.

We establish the required power, operating hours, charging windows and any motor starting loads. Battery capacity alone does not determine how long the site can run without the generator.

Designed for sites without a reliable mains supply

This approach can be explored for remote commercial premises, agricultural operations and sites where a mains connection is unavailable or impractical. Each system is designed around the site’s access, environment, electrical requirements and continuity needs.

Common questions

Can we stop using the generator completely?

That is not assumed. The generator may still be needed to recharge storage or supply sustained demand. We design the operating strategy around your available energy sources and continuity requirements.

Can you work with our existing generator?

Potentially. We first assess its output, condition, controls and compatibility with the proposed inverter and charging equipment.

Can you use our existing solar panels?

Yes, subject to an assessment of the existing equipment and its compatibility. We can integrate suitable existing solar with battery storage and generator support, or include a new solar installation where needed.

How much fuel will we save?

There is no single figure for every site. We need to understand the generator’s fuel use and the timing and size of the loads before estimating the benefit of storage.
