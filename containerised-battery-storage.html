Containerised battery storage

Built off site. Designed around your business.

We design, build and fit out energy storage containers to suit your project. From everyday energy management to dedicated backup, the equipment and layout are selected around your site and operating requirements.

Discuss Your Project →

What goes into the design

We work with you to establish the job your system needs to do: store surplus solar, manage electricity costs, support critical loads or make better use of an existing grid connection. These objectives guide the storage capacity, power requirements and controls.

Site access, electrical integration, serviceability and safety requirements are considered during the survey and design process. We use equipment from recognised brands including Victron Energy, Fogstar and Schneider Electric, with the final selection identified in your proposal.

Built and fitted out before arrival

We build and fit out the container off site, integrating equipment around the agreed design. Preparing the system before it reaches your premises helps reduce the amount of work and disruption on site.

Installation and commissioning follow, with handover information for your team. Each proposal defines the project scope and responsibilities so you know what is included.

A clear handover

  • Agreed system specification and installed equipment details
  • Commissioning records and operating guidance
  • Workmanship and equipment warranty information
  • Monitoring setup, care arrangements and support contacts

Care beyond installation

Your installation includes a two-year workmanship warranty, applicable manufacturer warranties for installed equipment, and one year of care including remote monitoring. After year one, optional annual subscriptions are available for monitoring, maintenance, or both.

The care arrangements define what is monitored and any agreed reviews, alerts or site visits. Read about support and warranties →

Common project questions

Is every container the same?

No. We build to suit the project. The equipment and layout depend on your energy use, site constraints and the purpose of the system.

Can a container work with our existing solar?

We assess the existing system and its integration requirements during design. Find out about battery storage for existing solar.

What information do you need to get started?

Your location, operating hours, project priorities and any planned changes are a useful start. Energy data helps with sizing, but you do not need it ready for an initial conversation.
