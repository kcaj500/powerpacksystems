Privacy information

This notice explains how Power Pack System Ltd, company number 17218077, VAT number 523 1033 47, Frome, UK, handles information provided through this website. For privacy enquiries, email info@powerpacksystems.co.uk.

Information you provide

We receive your name, business name, email address and the project details you choose to send. Optional information includes your phone number, site postcode, site status, solar installation details and energy-usage files. Please only upload information relevant to your enquiry.

Why we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, understand your site requirements and discuss or prepare a project proposal. For business enquiries, our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to prospective customers and assessing potential work. Where we take steps at your request towards a contract with you personally, we rely on that pre-contractual purpose where applicable.

How your enquiry reaches us

The form sends your details and any attachment to FormSubmit, which processes the submission and delivers it to our email address. Our email provider also processes the resulting message. FormSubmit uses spam protection and may present a CAPTCHA or confirmation page. Read FormSubmit’s privacy information for its processing practices.

FormSubmit states that submission details are retained in its archive for 30 days and that uploaded files are not retained in that archive. Copies delivered to our inbox are separate from this service archive.

How long information is needed

Enquiry information is used while we assess and respond to your request and conduct related follow-up. The period needed depends on whether the project proceeds, the ongoing business relationship and any applicable record-keeping or legal requirements. Contact us to ask about retention or request deletion of your enquiry information.

Website services

This website is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These services receive technical request information, such as your IP address, when your browser connects to them. FormSubmit receives your submission when you send the form. Provider processing may take place outside the UK.

We have not added advertising trackers or analytics to this website. External services, including FormSubmit’s spam checks, may use their own cookies or similar technology. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Google’s privacy policy.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask for access to your personal information, correction, deletion or restriction of its use, object to processing, or request portability where applicable. Contact us using the email above. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office.
