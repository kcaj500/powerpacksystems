Confidence beyond installation

Support & warranties

Every Power Pack installation includes a workmanship warranty, the applicable manufacturer warranties for the equipment installed, and one year of care including remote monitoring. We remain your point of contact after handover. After year one, you can choose an annual subscription for remote monitoring, maintenance, or both.

Workmanship warranty

Our workmanship is covered by a two-year warranty. The equipment we install is covered by its applicable manufacturer warranty, with terms and coverage set out in your project documentation.

One year of care included

Your first year includes remote monitoring for visibility of system performance, plus help with questions about operating your Power Pack. At handover, we explain what is monitored and how to contact us for support. Any scheduled reviews, alerts or site visits are defined in your project’s care arrangements. After year one, you can choose an annual subscription for monitoring, maintenance, or both.

Ongoing care options

After year one, remote monitoring and/or maintenance are available through an annual subscription. Choose monitoring, maintenance, or both, with the scope and any maintenance schedule agreed around your system and site.

Built for
long-term service

Our systems are designed with long-term performance and serviceability in mind. A well-maintained battery energy storage system can provide 15–20 years of service, depending on its specification, operating conditions and usage.

Battery capacity gradually reduces over time, and some components may require replacement during the system’s life. We explain the expected performance, maintenance requirements and applicable warranties for your installation as part of your proposal.

After your included first year of care, optional annual monitoring and/or maintenance subscriptions help keep your system operating effectively throughout its working life.
