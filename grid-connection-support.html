Grid connection support

More from the connection you already have.

Planning new equipment, EV (Electric Vehicle) charging or an expansion? Battery storage may help manage demand within your existing electricity connection before you commit to a costly upgrade.

Discuss Your Project →

Understand the constraint first

We assess your existing supply, half-hourly electricity usage and planned loads. The important question is how much power your site needs, when it needs it and how long its demand peaks last.

How storage can support demand peaks

A correctly sized battery can charge when demand is lower and supply energy during peaks. With suitable controls, this may help keep grid demand within the agreed import capacity.

Storage shifts energy use over time; it does not create additional energy. There must be enough opportunity to recharge between demand peaks. Where demand is persistently above the available supply, a connection upgrade may still be needed.

Working through the connection process

Where required, we handle the DNO (Distribution Network Operator) process and documentation. Whether an upgrade can be reduced or avoided depends on the site’s load profile, battery capacity, controls and the requirements of the DNO.

A storage proposal is not a substitute for the necessary connection assessment or approvals. We establish the route with you as part of the project design.

What helps us assess your site

  • Existing supply and agreed import capacity, where known
  • Previous 12 months of half-hourly usage for an established site
  • Planned equipment, operating hours and likely demand peaks
  • Any connection correspondence or upgrade proposal already received

You can start a conversation before all this information is available. New sites can use projected demand.

Common questions

Can you guarantee we will avoid an upgrade?

No. We assess whether storage is a practical option for your site and explain the constraints and assumptions behind the proposal.

Could this help with EV charging?

Potentially. Charger demand, usage patterns, available supply and recharge time all affect whether battery support is suitable.

Can the same system also reduce energy costs?

Yes — the same system can combine demand management with storage for new or existing solar to help reduce electricity costs. The operating strategy must still meet your site’s capacity constraints, and the savings depend on your generation, tariff and usage. Explore commercial battery storage →
