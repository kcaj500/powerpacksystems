A simple first step

From first conversation
to a sized system.

Start with a conversation about your priorities. We then review the information needed to understand where storage, solar or a combined system could create value.

  1. 01

    Discuss your project

    Tell us about your site, priorities and planned changes. You do not need energy data ready to start the conversation.

  2. 02

    Energy & tariff analysis

    We review consumption, peak demand, tariffs, solar generation and grid constraints. For established sites, the previous 12 months of half-hourly data is ideal; new sites can use projected demand.

  3. 03

    Savings proposal

    You receive a build-to-suit system proposal with projected electricity-cost savings, an estimated payback period and the key assumptions behind the figures. We set out the proposed equipment and costs so you can assess the investment against your business priorities.

  4. 04

    Survey, DNO & design

    We confirm site conditions, access, electrical integration and safety requirements, then finalise the design and coordinate the necessary DNO process where required.

  5. 05

    Build, install & hand over

    We build and fit out your container off site, then install and commission the system at your premises. We provide your warranty and handover documentation and set up the remote monitoring included in your first year of care. Optional annual subscriptions for monitoring, maintenance, or both are available after year one.
