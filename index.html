Grid connection holding you back?

Avoid an
unnecessary
DNO upgrade.

Planning new equipment, EV (Electric Vehicle) charging or an expansion, but your existing electricity connection is already close to its limit? A larger grid connection can mean significant cost and long lead times. Before committing to an upgrade, it is worth understanding how your site actually uses power.

A correctly sized battery can charge when demand is lower and support your site during demand peaks. Combined with suitable controls, this can help keep grid demand within your agreed import capacity and, where the load profile allows, reduce or avoid the need for a larger connection.

We work with you to assess your existing supply, half-hourly usage data and planned loads, then design a build-to-suit solution. Where required, we handle the DNO (Distribution Network Operator) process and documentation. Whether an upgrade can be avoided depends on your site's demand, battery capacity and the requirements of the DNO (Distribution Network Operator).

A simple first step

From first conversation
to a sized system.

Start with a conversation about your priorities. We then review the information needed to understand where storage, solar or a combined system could create value.

  1. 01

    Discuss your project

    Tell us about your site, priorities and planned changes. You do not need energy data ready to start the conversation.

  2. 02

    Energy & tariff analysis

    We review consumption, peak demand, tariffs, solar generation and grid constraints. For established sites, the previous 12 months of half-hourly data is ideal; new sites can use projected demand.

  3. 03

    Savings proposal

    You receive a build-to-suit system proposal with projected electricity-cost savings, an estimated payback period and the key assumptions behind the figures. We set out the proposed equipment and costs so you can assess the investment against your business priorities.

  4. 04

    Survey, DNO & design

    We confirm site conditions, access, electrical integration and safety requirements, then finalise the design and coordinate the necessary DNO (Distribution Network Operator) process where required.

  5. 05

    Build, install & hand over

    We build and fit out your container off site, then install and commission the system at your premises. We provide your warranty and handover documentation and set up the remote monitoring included in your first year of care. Optional annual subscriptions for monitoring, maintenance, or both are available after year one.
