Commercial UPS & backup systems

Resilience around the loads that matter.

A Power Pack can be designed as a UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) and backup system for defined critical loads. We establish what must remain operational, the required backup duration and how the system will integrate with your site.

Discuss Your Project →

Start with your critical operations

A backup project begins with the equipment and processes your business needs to protect. We work with you to identify critical loads, their power requirements and the consequences of an interruption. This helps establish the scope before equipment is selected.

Agree how backup should behave

Backup duration, starting loads and acceptable interruption times affect the design. Where uninterrupted operation is required, the proposed UPS arrangement must be specified for those loads. The terms battery storage and backup do not by themselves establish a no-break supply.

We review electrical integration, site constraints and any existing backup equipment during design. Your proposal sets out the intended operation and the equipment selected for it.

Designed for resilience

A dedicated backup system can be worthwhile even when electricity-cost savings are not the primary objective. Where a project also includes solar storage or tariff optimisation, those functions need to be considered alongside the required backup reserve.

Built off site, handed over clearly

We build and fit out the container off site, then install and commission the system. Handover covers the agreed specification, operating guidance and support arrangements. Explore container design and build →

Common questions

Does every battery system provide UPS protection?

No. The required changeover performance and protected loads must be part of the design. We assess the backup requirements of your project before proposing equipment.

How long can the system provide backup?

That depends on the usable stored energy and the loads being supplied. We establish the required duration with you and size the proposed system around it.

Do we need solar?

No. A dedicated backup system can be designed without solar generation. The charging and operating arrangements are agreed for your site.
